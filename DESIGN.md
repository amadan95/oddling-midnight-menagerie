# ODDLING design system

Midnight Menagerie treats each ODDLING as the featured act on a contemporary dance-hall handbill. The entire surface is fibrous cream paper with deep plum as the essential ink and hot ochre as a printed accent. A scalloped frame closes every viewport; stars, ticket tabs, and billing lines replace generic interface chrome.

Bowlby One SC handles the wordmark and names, League Gothic carries headline-scale billing, and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono carries practical labels. Creature families are face-free plum silhouettes with ochre gesture marks; their shape remains deterministic while the printed world stays strictly two-ink. Animation is limited to ink settling, posture changes, and the pair dance. Reduced-motion users receive the finished print without movement.

The practical creation controls are part of the handbill: a printed progress line shows Shape, Rhythm, and Warmth; selected presets become filled tickets; hold warmth is a pressable sunburst with a matching labeled range input. Every essential control has a 44px target and plum focus outline. The screen silhouette, local program thumbnails, and portrait exports derive from one recipe-driven SVG grammar so the printed creature remains the same wherever it appears.
