# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who share unusual websites, digital art, personality quizzes, and playful discoveries in group chats. They arrive curious and should understand the experience without an introductory explanation.

## Product Purpose

ODDLING lets a visitor grow a small impossible creature from drawing, tapping, and holding gestures. Success is a creature that feels personally authored, creates an attractive portrait, and gives the visitor a natural reason to invite a friend to make its companion.

## Positioning

Unlike an open drawing toy or conventional character generator, ODDLING turns a short embodied gesture sequence into a persistent creature identity and a two-creature encounter.

## Operating Context

The experience is mobile-first, account-free, brief, and shareable through portable links. Visitors may arrive from a message containing another person's specimen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The product includes gesture creation, accessible preset controls, four deterministic creature families, individual reveals, pair encounters, portrait export, fragment-based share links, optional sound, reduced motion, and a browser-local collection. It excludes accounts, feeds, uploads, payments, runtime AI, multiplayer synchronization, and backend storage.

## Brand Commitments

The name is ODDLING. The voice is gentle, curious, concise, and literal about what gestures influence. It must avoid personality, compatibility, and emotional-health claims. Creatures are strange, sculptural, and lovable without becoming conventional cartoon mascots.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing implementation supplies procedural creature artwork, approved product copy, deterministic recipes, and working creation/share flows. There are no testimonials, usage benchmarks, or commercial claims to present.

## Product Principles

- Put the living artifact before explanation.
- Make every interaction feel causally connected to the resulting creature.
- Reward creation with an object worth saving outside the site.
- Turn sharing into a new act of creation rather than passive promotion.
- Keep the experience playful without scores, pressure, or diagnosis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every gesture has a labeled keyboard-accessible alternative. The experience preserves meaning without sound, supports reduced motion, and maintains readable contrast and 44px minimum interactive targets.
